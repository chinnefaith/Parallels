About ShellScan

AI-powered shell identification and classification workspace.

ShellScan helps researchers, students, and coastal communities identify and classify shell species using artificial intelligence. Upload a single image or a batch of images, and get fast, accurate results with confidence scores and processing history.

Prediction transparency

Each prediction includes confidence score and the full probability distribution across classes. This helps you understand the model's certainty.

Predictions are estimates, not absolute truth. Always verify important results with expert references.
◫

For learning and reference

ShellScan is built primarily to assist students and enthusiasts. Use it as a reference tool to learn about different species, not as a replacement for expert identification.

Browse shell library → View history →
△

System limitations

While ShellScan is highly accurate, keep a few important limitations in mind.

  • Image quality matters — blurry or cropped shells may reduce accuracy.
  • Limited to trained classes — species not included in the dataset may be misclassified.
  • Verify results — use predictions as a reference, not as a substitute for expert identification.
